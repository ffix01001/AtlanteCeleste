## Science

BepiColombo is a joint mission of ESA and the Japanese space agency JAXA made of two probes traveling hooked together: MPO, which will study the surface and interior of Mercury, and Mio, devoted to its magnetic field. Braking and falling towards the planet closest to the Sun takes enormous amounts of energy: BepiColombo uses ion engines and no fewer than nine planetary flybys (one of the Earth, two of Venus, six of Mercury).

## History

It was launched on 20 October 2018 from Kourou. Orbit insertion around Mercury, postponed because of a problem with the thrusters, is scheduled for the end of 2026; science operations will begin in 2027.

## Fun facts

- The name honors Giuseppe "Bepi" Colombo, the Paduan scientist who explained Mercury's rotation and invented the maneuver that allowed Mariner 10 to fly past it three times.
- Several instruments are Italian, including the SIMBIO-SYS camera, the ISA accelerometer, the MORE radio science experiment and the SERENA particle detector.
