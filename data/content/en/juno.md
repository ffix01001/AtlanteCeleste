## Science

Juno studies Jupiter from a very elongated polar orbit, which makes it whiz a few thousand km above the clouds and then carries it far from the most intense radiation belts. It discovered that the planet's core is "diluted", photographed the cyclones arranged in geometric figures at the poles and mapped the strongest magnetic field among the planets. In recent years it has also flown close to Ganymede, Europa and Io.

## History

It was launched on 5 August 2011 and entered orbit around Jupiter in July 2016. It is the first solar-powered probe to work so far from the Sun, with three large panels 9 meters long. The mission has been extended several times. Italy takes part with the JIRAM infrared spectrometer and the KaT radio science instrument.

## Fun facts

- Three aluminum LEGO figurines travel on board: Galileo Galilei, Jupiter and Juno, along with a plaque dedicated to Galileo provided by the Italian Space Agency.
- The name comes from Jupiter's wife, who in the myth could see through the clouds the god hid behind.
