## Science

Ariel is the brightest of the large moons of Uranus and has the youngest surface: few large craters, and long valleys and canyons with smooth floors, perhaps filled by flows of ice. Observations from ground-based and space telescopes have detected carbon dioxide ice on its surface.

## History

Discovered by William Lassell on 24 October 1851, together with Umbriel. The name comes both from the spirit in Shakespeare's *The Tempest* and from a sylph in Alexander Pope's *The Rape of the Lock*.

## Fun facts

- Like all the moons of Uranus, Ariel lives through the planet's extreme seasons: decades of light followed by decades of darkness.
- Voyager 2 photographed only its southern hemisphere.
