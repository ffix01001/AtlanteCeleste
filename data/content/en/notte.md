## Science

Nix is a small moon of Pluto, about 50 km long. It tumbles chaotically, because it is tossed around by the changing gravitational field of the Pluto–Charon pair.

## History

Discovered in 2005 with the Hubble Space Telescope, together with Hydra, during the preparations for the New Horizons mission.

## Fun facts

- In mythology Nyx (Night) is the mother of Charon. The initials of Nix and Hydra, N and H, recall those of New Horizons.
