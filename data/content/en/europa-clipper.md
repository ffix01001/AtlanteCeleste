## Science

Europa Clipper is the largest planetary probe NASA has ever built: its solar panels, unfolded, span about 30 meters. Its goal is to find out whether the ocean hidden beneath the ice of the moon Europa could host life. It will not enter orbit around Europa but around Jupiter, flying past the moon 49 times at altitudes as low as 25 km.

## History

It was launched on 14 October 2024 on a Falcon Heavy rocket. It has already used the gravity of Mars (March 2025) and will use the Earth's at the end of 2026 to reach Jupiter in April 2030.

## Fun facts

- It carries a plaque with the poem *In Praise of Mystery* by Ada Limón and the word "water" spoken in more than a hundred languages, turned into waveforms.
- The electronics travel inside an aluminum and titanium "vault" to protect them from Jupiter's radiation.
