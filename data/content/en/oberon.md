## Science

Oberon is the outermost of the large moons of Uranus (about 1,520 km): an old, cratered surface, some craters with floors covered in dark material and a mountain rising about 11 km. It spends part of its orbit outside the magnetosphere of Uranus.

## History

Discovered by William Herschel on 11 January 1787. The name is that of the king of the fairies in Shakespeare's *A Midsummer Night's Dream*.

## Fun facts

- Oberon and Titania were the first moons of Uranus to be discovered, but for decades no other astronomer managed to see them.
