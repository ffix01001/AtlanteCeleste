## Science

C/2020 F3 (NEOWISE) was the brightest comet seen from the northern hemisphere since Hale-Bopp. Coming from the outskirts of the Solar System, it passed perihelion on 3 July 2020 at less than 0.3 times the Earth–Sun distance, and in mid-July it showed a beautiful curved tail to the naked eye.

## History

It was discovered on 27 March 2020 by NASA's NEOWISE infrared space telescope, designed to search for near-Earth asteroids. In the middle of the pandemic, it became for many a small show to watch from the balcony.

## Fun facts

- Its orbital period after this passage is estimated at about 6,700 years: none of us will see it again.
- Many photographed it above the Dolomites and the Italian lakes on July evenings in 2020.
