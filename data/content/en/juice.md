## Science

JUICE (JUpiter ICy moons Explorer) is ESA's large mission to Jupiter and its icy moons. It will study Europa, Callisto and above all Ganymede, around which it will enter orbit in December 2034: it will be the first probe to orbit a moon other than ours.

## History

It was launched on 14 April 2023 on an Ariane 5 rocket. In August 2024 it performed the first combined flyby of the Moon and the Earth in history, then used Venus (August 2025) and the Earth again in 2026, to reach Jupiter in July 2031.

## Fun facts

- Italy plays a leading role: the RIME radar, which will "see" beneath the ice, the MAJIS spectrometer and the 3GM radio science experiment.
- The antenna of the RIME radar is 16 meters long: during deployment it got stuck and was freed by shaking it with small motors.
