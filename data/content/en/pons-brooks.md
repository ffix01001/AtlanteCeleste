## Science

12P/Pons-Brooks is a Halley-type comet that returns about every 71 years. In 2023, while approaching the Sun, it had a series of outbursts that gave it a shape with two "horns": the newspapers nicknamed it the "devil comet". At perihelion, in April 2024, it was visible with binoculars.

## History

It was discovered on 12 July 1812 in Marseille by Jean-Louis Pons, one of the greatest comet discoverers in history, who later directed the Specola observatory in Florence. It was found again in 1883 by the American William Robert Brooks.

## Fun facts

- Pons started out as the doorkeeper of the Marseille observatory and went on to discover dozens of comets.
- Its next return is expected around 2095.
