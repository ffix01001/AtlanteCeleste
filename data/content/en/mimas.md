## Science

Mimas is the smallest of Saturn's moons large enough to be nearly spherical (about 400 km). An impact dug out the Herschel crater, 130 km across, a third of the moon's diameter: the blow came close to shattering it. Mimas is also "responsible" for the Cassini Division in the rings, thanks to an orbital resonance.

In 2024 a study based on Cassini data showed that the small wobbles in its orbit are explained by an ocean hidden under the crust, surprisingly young: perhaps only 5–15 million years old.

## History

Discovered by William Herschel on 17 September 1789 with his large 40-foot telescope. Photographed in detail by the Voyagers and above all by Cassini.

## Fun facts

- With the Herschel crater, Mimas looks strikingly like the "Death Star" from *Star Wars*, a film released before the probes photographed it up close.
- Cassini's thermal maps show a "Pac-Man"-shaped pattern.
