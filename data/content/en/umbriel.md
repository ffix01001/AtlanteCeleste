## Science

Umbriel is the darkest of the large moons of Uranus: it reflects only about a quarter of the light it receives. Its surface is old and full of craters. A bright ring stands out, "Wunda", on the floor of a crater near the equator, whose nature is still unknown.

## History

Discovered by William Lassell on 24 October 1851. The name comes from the melancholy spirit in Alexander Pope's *The Rape of the Lock*.

## Fun facts

- Umbriel and Ariel are almost the same size, but one is very dark and the other bright: one of the small open puzzles for scientists.
