## Science

Tethys is an icy moon of about 1,060 km, with a density just above that of water: it is made almost entirely of ice. It is crossed by Ithaca Chasma, a valley about 2,000 km long, and bears the mark of the large crater Odysseus, 450 km across.

Tethys shares its orbit with two small "trojan" moons, Telesto and Calypso, which lead and trail it by 60°.

## History

Discovered by Giovanni Domenico Cassini on 21 March 1684 at the Paris Observatory, together with Dione. Cassini called the four moons of Saturn he had discovered *Sidera Lodoicea*, "Louis's stars", in honor of Louis XIV.

## Fun facts

- The names of the craters and valleys of Tethys come from Homer's *Odyssey*.
- Cassini's images show thin reddish streaks of still unknown origin.
