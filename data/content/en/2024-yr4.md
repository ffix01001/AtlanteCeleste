## Science

2024 YR4 is a near-Earth asteroid about 60 meters across, roughly the size of the object that devastated the Tunguska forest in Siberia in 1908. Measurements by the James Webb telescope, in March 2025, made it possible to estimate its size precisely.

## History

It was discovered on 27 December 2024 by the ATLAS network in Chile. In the following weeks the probability that it would hit the Earth on 22 December 2032 rose to about 3%, the highest value ever recorded for an object of this size. At the end of February 2025 new observations reduced the risk for the Earth practically to zero. According to estimates from mid-2025, however, a small probability remained, around 4%, of an impact on the Moon in 2032.

## Fun facts

- A possible lunar impact would be harmless to us and scientifically precious: we could watch a crater being born live.
- It is an excellent example of how asteroid monitoring works: the probabilities go up and down as observations narrow down the orbit.
