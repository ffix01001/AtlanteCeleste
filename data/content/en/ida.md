## Science

Ida is an elongated main-belt asteroid about 60 km long, belonging to the Koronis family. It is the first asteroid in which a moon was discovered: Dactyl, a small body about 1.4 km across.

## History

It was discovered on 29 September 1884 by Johann Palisa in Vienna. On 28 August 1993 the Galileo probe, on its way to Jupiter, flew past it; analyzing the images, scientists found Dactyl.

## Fun facts

- In mythology Ida is the nymph who raised Zeus on Mount Ida, inhabited by the Dactyls.
