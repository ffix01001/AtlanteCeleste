## Science

Dione, about 1,120 km across, is a moon of ice and rock with one hemisphere crossed by bright ice cliffs, which in the first images looked like wispy filaments. It too has two trojan moons, Helene and Polydeuces. Cassini detected a very thin oxygen atmosphere, and gravity data suggest a deep ocean beneath the crust.

## History

Discovered by Giovanni Domenico Cassini on 21 March 1684. Studied up close by the Voyagers and by Cassini, which flew past it for the last time in 2015.

## Fun facts

- Dione's bright "cliffs" are ice walls hundreds of meters high, formed by tectonic fractures.
- Dione is in resonance with Enceladus and helps keep its interior warm.
