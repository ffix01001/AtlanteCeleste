## Science

Lucy is the first mission to Jupiter's trojan asteroids, two swarms of primitive bodies that share the planet's orbit, 60° ahead of and 60° behind it. They are considered "fossils" of planet formation. On its journey Lucy will visit a record number of different asteroids.

## History

It was launched on 16 October 2021. In November 2023 it flew past the small asteroid Dinkinesh, discovering that it has a moon, Selam, itself made of two joined lobes. In April 2025 it passed by the asteroid Donaldjohanson. The first trojan, Eurybates, is expected in 2027.

## Fun facts

- The name comes from the Lucy fossil, a human ancestor discovered in Ethiopia in 1974, which in turn owes its name to the Beatles song *Lucy in the Sky with Diamonds*.
- One of the solar panels did not fully open after launch: the team managed to lock it in an almost complete position with a delicate remote maneuver.
