## Science

OSIRIS-APEX is the new life of the OSIRIS-REx probe. After bringing the samples of the asteroid Bennu back to Earth, it was sent back into space towards the asteroid Apophis, which it will reach in 2029, right after its close pass by the Earth. It will study how the Earth's gravity has changed it and use its own thrusters to stir up the surface dust.

## History

The probe was launched on 8 September 2016 as OSIRIS-REx. In October 2020 it collected material from Bennu, and on 24 September 2023 it released the capsule with the samples over Utah. A few minutes later it fired its engines towards its new destination and changed its name: APEX stands for *Apophis Explorer*.

## Fun facts

- Reusing a probe already in space costs a fraction of a new mission.
- During the journey it will pass closer to the Sun several times than originally planned: the team had to adapt its procedures to protect it from the heat.
