## Science

Nereid has one of the most elongated orbits of all known moons: its distance from Neptune varies between about 1.4 and 9.7 million km. It is about 350 km across and is perhaps a captured body or an object displaced when Neptune captured Triton.

## History

Discovered by Gerard Kuiper on 1 May 1949. It was the last moon of Neptune found before the era of space probes.

## Fun facts

- The Nereids were the sea nymphs in Neptune's retinue.
