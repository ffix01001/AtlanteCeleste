## Science

Didymos is a double near-Earth asteroid: the main body is about 760 meters across, and around it orbits Dimorphos, a small moon about 150 meters across. It was the target of the first planetary defense experiment in history.

On 26 September 2022 NASA's DART probe deliberately crashed into Dimorphos at about 6 km/s. The impact shortened the moon's orbital period by about 33 minutes, from 11 hours and 55 minutes to about 11 hours and 22 minutes: proof that an asteroid can be deflected.

## History

Didymos was discovered in 1996 by the Spacewatch program. DART's impact was photographed by LICIACube, a small Italian satellite of the Italian Space Agency built by Argotec of Turin, which flew a few minutes behind the probe. The European mission Hera is on its way to study the effects of the impact up close.

## Fun facts

- "Didymos" means "twin" in Greek.
- The impact flung dozens of boulders into space and probably changed the shape of Dimorphos.
