## Science

Arrokoth is the most distant object ever explored up close: a Kuiper Belt body 36 km long, made of two flattened lobes gently joined together, like a squashed snowman. It is an intact fossil from the origins of the Solar System: the two lobes merged at low speed, without a violent collision. Its surface is red and contains frozen methanol.

## History

Discovered in 2014 with the Hubble telescope, while searching for a target for New Horizons after Pluto. On 1 January 2019 the probe flew past it at about 3,500 km, more than 6.5 billion km from the Sun.

## Fun facts

- The name means "sky" in the language of the Powhatan and Algonquian peoples of Chesapeake Bay.
- During the flyby, New Horizons' signals took more than six hours to reach the Earth.
