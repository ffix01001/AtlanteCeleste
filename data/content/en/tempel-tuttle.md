## Science

Comet Tempel-Tuttle is the parent of the Leonids, the November shower. It returns to perihelion about every 33 years and, in the years close to its passage, the Earth can meet particularly dense dust trails: in those cases the Leonids become real meteor "storms".

## History

It was discovered independently by Wilhelm Tempel (December 1865) and Horace Tuttle (January 1866). The Leonid storm of 13 November 1833, with tens of thousands of meteors per hour visible in the United States, marked the birth of the scientific study of shooting stars.

## Fun facts

- In 1966 some observers in America counted dozens of meteors per second for a few minutes.
- Its last perihelion was in 1998; the next will be in 2031.
