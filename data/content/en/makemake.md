## Science

Makemake is the second brightest object in the Kuiper Belt after Pluto. It is about 1,430 km across, with a reddish surface of frozen methane and nitrogen. It completes an orbit in about 305 years.

In 2016 the Hubble telescope discovered a small, very dark moon around it, nicknamed MK2. More recent observations suggest that its surface might have areas warmer or more active than one would expect from such a cold world.

## History

It was discovered on 31 March 2005, a few days after Easter, by Mike Brown's team at Palomar Observatory. That is why the team nicknamed it "Easter Bunny", and the official name was chosen accordingly: Makemake is the creator god of the Rapa Nui people of Easter Island.

## Fun facts

- It is the only dwarf planet named after a Polynesian deity of Easter Island, famous for its moai statues.
- In 2011 Makemake passed in front of a star: the observation showed that it has no global atmosphere, at least at that time.
- Its moon is so dark and so close to it that it took Hubble to discover it.
