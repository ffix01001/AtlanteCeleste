## Science

Vesta is the second most massive body in the asteroid belt and a failed protoplanet: it has an iron core, a mantle and a crust, like the rocky planets. At its south pole lies Rheasilvia, an impact crater about 500 km across with a central peak more than 20 km high, one of the highest landforms in the Solar System.

That impact threw large amounts of rock into space: part of it has reached the Earth as meteorites (the so-called HEDs), about one in twenty of those we see fall.

## History

It was discovered by Heinrich Olbers on 29 March 1807; the name was chosen by Carl Friedrich Gauss. NASA's Dawn probe studied it from orbit between 2011 and 2012, before moving on to Ceres.

## Fun facts

- It is the only asteroid that, under the best conditions, can be seen with the naked eye.
- We have samples of Vesta without ever having gone there: they are the HED meteorites kept in museums all over the world.
