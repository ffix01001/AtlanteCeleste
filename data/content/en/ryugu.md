## Science

Ryugu is a near-Earth asteroid about 900 meters across, shaped like a spinning top, very dark and rich in carbon. It is a "rubble pile" that has remained almost unchanged since the origins of the Solar System. Amino acids and uracil, one of the bases of RNA, were found in its samples.

## History

Discovered in 1999 by the LINEAR program. The Japanese probe Hayabusa2 reached it in 2018, set the first small rovers hopping on an asteroid and in 2019 fired a copper projectile to create an artificial crater and collect material from below the surface. On 6 December 2020 the capsule brought 5.4 grams of samples back to Earth.

## Fun facts

- The name comes from the "Dragon Palace" of a Japanese fairy tale, from which the fisherman Urashima Tarō returns carrying a mysterious box, just like the probe.
