## Science

Phoebe is a dark moon about 210 km across that orbits Saturn backwards, very far from the planet. It is probably an object born in the outer regions of the Solar System and captured by Saturn. The dust torn from its surface forms a gigantic, almost invisible ring, the largest in the Solar System, discovered in 2009 by the Spitzer infrared telescope.

## History

It was the first moon discovered by photography: William Henry Pickering found it in 1899 on plates taken at Arequipa, in Peru. Cassini flew past it in June 2004, even before entering orbit around Saturn.

## Fun facts

- The dust of the Phoebe ring is thought to be the same that darkens one hemisphere of Iapetus.
- Phoebe rotates in a little over 9 hours.
