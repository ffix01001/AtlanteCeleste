## Science

Gaia is the ESA satellite that created the most precise map of the Milky Way ever made: positions, distances, motions and colors of almost two billion stars, as well as millions of galaxies, quasars and asteroids. It measured positions with a precision comparable to what you would need to see a coin on the Moon.

## History

It was launched on 19 December 2013 and observed the sky from an orbit around the L2 point for more than ten years. The third catalog (DR3) was published in 2022; the fourth, with all the data from the first five and a half years, is expected around the end of 2026. On 27 March 2025, its mission complete, Gaia was moved into an orbit around the Sun and switched off for good.

## Fun facts

- Gaia's data are the basis on which this Atlas will one day be able to map the whole Milky Way.
- Gaia discovered "runaway" stars flung out by supernova explosions, streams of stars from ancient galaxies swallowed by ours and even a few dormant black holes.
