## Science

Pioneer 11 flew past Jupiter in December 1974, passing about 43,000 km above the clouds, and then was the first probe to visit Saturn, on 1 September 1979, paving the way for the Voyagers. Today it travels without contact towards the constellation Aquila.

## History

It was launched on 6 April 1973. The last useful contact dates back to 1995, when its generators could no longer power the instruments.

## Fun facts

- Pioneer 11 also carries the plaque with the message for possible other civilizations.
- It used Jupiter's gravity to change its orbital plane and reach Saturn: one of the first large-scale "gravitational slingshots".
