## Science

Parker Solar Probe is the probe that "touches" the Sun. Thanks to repeated passes near Venus it has shrunk its orbit until, on 24 December 2024, it came within about 6.1 million km of the solar surface. At that point it was traveling at about 690,000 km/h, the highest speed ever reached by a human-made object.

A heat shield of carbon composite, about 11 cm thick, withstands temperatures of about 1,400 °C, while the instruments behind it stay at room temperature. In 2021 the probe entered the solar corona for the first time.

## History

It was launched on 12 August 2018. It is the first NASA mission named after a person still alive when it was announced: the astrophysicist Eugene Parker, who in the 1950s had predicted the existence of the solar wind. Parker watched the launch and died in 2022.

## Fun facts

- On board is a memory card with the names of more than a million people and a photo of Eugene Parker.
- At that speed you would go from Rome to Milan in less than three seconds.
