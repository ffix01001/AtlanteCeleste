## Science

The Sagittarius-Carina Arm is the first great arm inside the Sun's orbit, a few thousand light years from us towards the center of the Galaxy. From the northern hemisphere we see it in the summer constellations Sagittarius, Scutum and Serpens; from the southern hemisphere it continues in Carina.

It is an arm very rich in gas and young stars. Here lie the Eagle Nebula with the "Pillars of Creation", the Lagoon Nebula, the Omega Nebula and, in the southern part, the Carina Nebula with the gigantic star Eta Carinae.

## History

It was one of the first arms to be recognized, already in the 1950s, thanks to observations of neutral hydrogen at the 21 cm wavelength and to the distribution of young O and B stars. The most recent parallax measurements indicate that its stretch near the Sun is almost circular, while towards Carina it winds more sharply.

## Fun facts

- Many of the nebulae most photographed by Italian amateur astronomers on summer nights belong to this arm.
- In the Atlante Celeste model the observed stretches follow the parameters of Reid and collaborators (2019).
