## Science

1I/ʻOumuamua is the first object from another star system that we have ever observed. Its orbit was strongly hyperbolic: it entered the Solar System at about 26 km/s and left, never to return.

Its changes in brightness point to an extremely elongated shape, perhaps 10 times longer than it is wide, or flat like a pancake. As it moved away it showed a small acceleration that gravity alone cannot explain, but without the gas tail typical of comets. Among the proposed explanations: the release of molecular hydrogen trapped in ice.

## History

It was discovered on 19 October 2017 by Robert Weryk with the Pan-STARRS1 telescope in Hawaii, when it had already passed the Sun and was moving away. It was observed for a few weeks before it became too faint.

## Fun facts

- The Hawaiian name means roughly "a messenger from afar arriving first".
- A highly controversial hypothesis, supported by a few researchers, described it as an artificial sail; most astronomers consider it a natural fragment.
- It started a new designation: the "I" stands for "interstellar".
