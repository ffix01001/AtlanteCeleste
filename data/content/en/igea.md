## Science

Hygiea is the fourth largest body in the asteroid belt (about 430 km). Images from the Very Large Telescope in 2019 showed that it is almost spherical: for this reason it might be classified as a dwarf planet, the smallest in the Solar System.

## History

It was discovered on 12 April 1849 by Annibale de Gasparis at the Capodimonte Observatory, in Naples. At first it was called "Hygiea Borbonica", in honor of the ruling house of Naples. De Gasparis discovered nine asteroids in all.

## Fun facts

- Despite its size, Hygiea is very dark and has never been visited by a probe.
