## Science

3I/ATLAS is the third known interstellar object and the fastest: its orbit has an eccentricity of about 6, the most hyperbolic ever measured, and it will leave the Solar System at about 58 km/s. It is an active comet, rich in carbon dioxide, in whose coma nickel vapor has also been detected.

From its speed and direction it is estimated that it may come from the oldest regions of the Milky Way: it might be older than the Solar System, perhaps by several billion years.

## History

It was discovered on 1 July 2025 by the ATLAS network telescope at Río Hurtado, in Chile. It passed perihelion at the end of October 2025, at about 1.36 times the Earth–Sun distance, when from Earth it was almost behind the Sun; during that period the probes orbiting Mars observed it up close. In December 2025 it reached its minimum distance from the Earth, about 1.8 times the Earth–Sun distance, and in March 2026 it passed near Jupiter.

## Fun facts

- It follows a retrograde orbit almost aligned with the plane of the planets: a very rare case.
- When it left its own star, the Sun and the Earth may not have existed yet.
- In the Atlas you can follow its hyperbolic trajectory: it is an open orbit that never closes.
