## Science

Titania is the largest moon of Uranus (about 1,580 km). Its surface is marked by huge canyons, the largest of which, Messina Chasma, is about 1,500 km long. Fractures of this kind suggest that the interior expanded in the past, perhaps because an ocean froze.

## History

Discovered by William Herschel on 11 January 1787, the same day as Oberon. The name is that of the queen of the fairies in Shakespeare's *A Midsummer Night's Dream*.

## Fun facts

- Titania's canyons are named after places in Shakespeare's plays: Messina comes from *Much Ado About Nothing*, set in Sicily.
- In 2001 Titania passed in front of a star: this showed that it has no significant atmosphere.
