## Science

Pallas is the third largest body in the asteroid belt (about 510 km). Its orbit is unusually tilted, almost 35° with respect to the plane of the planets, which makes it hard for a probe to reach. Images from the Very Large Telescope in 2020 showed a surface full of craters, like a golf ball.

## History

It was the second asteroid discovered, on 28 March 1802, by the German physician and astronomer Heinrich Olbers in Bremen, while he was trying to find Ceres again.

## Fun facts

- The element palladium, discovered in 1803, is named after Pallas.
- For a few decades Pallas was considered a planet, like Ceres.
