## Science

Pioneer 10 was the first probe to cross the asteroid belt and fly past Jupiter, in December 1973, showing how intense its radiation belts were. Then it continued outwards: today it travels silently beyond 130 times the Earth–Sun distance, towards the star Aldebaran.

## History

It was launched on 3 March 1972. Its last faint signal was received on 23 January 2003. For years scientists studied a small unexplained deceleration of the Pioneers, the "Pioneer anomaly": in 2012 it was shown to be caused by heat given off unevenly by the probe itself.

## Fun facts

- It carries a plaque designed by Carl Sagan, Frank Drake and Linda Salzman Sagan, with a man, a woman and the position of the Sun shown relative to 14 pulsars.
- It will reach the neighborhood of Aldebaran in about two million years.
