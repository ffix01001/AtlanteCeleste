## Science

NASA's Psyche probe is headed for the metal asteroid Psyche, perhaps the exposed core of an ancient protoplanet. It uses electric propulsion: Hall-effect thrusters that expel xenon ions with a tiny but continuous thrust: according to NASA, roughly the pressure you feel holding three coins in your hand.

## History

It was launched on 13 October 2023 on a Falcon Heavy rocket. In May 2026 it used the gravity of Mars, and it will reach the asteroid in 2029.

## Fun facts

- On board is a deep-space laser communication experiment: in December 2023 it transmitted to Earth a video of a cat, Taters, from about 31 million km away.
- The names of the probe and of the asteroid come from Psyche of the myth, a mortal loved by Eros.
