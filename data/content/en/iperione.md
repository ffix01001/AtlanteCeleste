## Science

Hyperion looks like a sponge: an irregular body of about 360 × 266 × 205 km, full of deep craters with sharp rims. Its density is about half that of water, so it is mostly empty space, a porous heap of ice.

It is also famous for its chaotic rotation: because of its irregular shape and the influence of Titan, its axis wobbles unpredictably.

## History

Discovered in September 1848 by William Cranch Bond and George Phillips Bond in the United States and, independently, by William Lassell in England. Cassini photographed it up close in 2005.

## Fun facts

- It is one of the largest non-spherical bodies in the Solar System.
- When Cassini flew through it, Hyperion's surface released a beam of charged particles: a kind of long-distance "electric shock".
