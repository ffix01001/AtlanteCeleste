## Science

Solar Orbiter studies the Sun up close, down to about a quarter of the Earth–Sun distance, with a unique combination of telescopes and instruments that measure the solar wind where the probe is. Thanks to flybys of Venus its orbit tilts more and more with respect to the solar equator: in 2025 it provided the first images ever of the Sun's poles.

## History

It is an ESA mission in collaboration with NASA, launched on 10 February 2020. In its first images it discovered tiny flares scattered all over the surface, nicknamed "campfires".

## Fun facts

- The Metis coronagraph, which observes the corona by blocking the Sun's disk, was built in Italy under the leadership of INAF.
- The heat shield has to withstand sunlight about 13 times more intense than what reaches the Earth.
