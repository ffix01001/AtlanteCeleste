## Science

Proteus is Neptune's second largest moon (about 420 km) and one of the darkest in the Solar System. It is considered close to the upper limit for a non-spherical body: a little bigger, and its own gravity would round it.

## History

It was discovered by Voyager 2 in 1989. Despite its size, it is too dark and too close to Neptune's glare to have been seen from Earth before.

## Fun facts

- Proteus is larger than Nereid, but it was discovered forty years later: its closeness to Neptune hid it.
