## Science

Callisto has the most heavily cratered surface in the Solar System: an ancient world, practically unchanged for 4 billion years, with no volcanoes and no tectonics. The Valhalla impact basin, with its concentric rings, spans about 3,800 km.

It is the outermost of the Galilean moons and does not take part in the resonance that heats Io, Europa and Ganymede. The magnetic measurements of the Galileo probe nevertheless suggest a deep ocean beneath the crust.

## History

Discovered by Galileo on 7 January 1610. The name comes from a nymph loved by Zeus and turned into a she-bear. NASA studies have pointed to Callisto as an ideal base for future human exploration of the Jupiter system, because it lies outside the planet's most intense radiation belts.

## Fun facts

- Callisto is almost as large as Mercury, but it is about half ice.
- On Callisto the radiation dose is a small fraction of what you would receive on Europa or Io.
- Many craters are named after heroes and places of Norse mythology.
