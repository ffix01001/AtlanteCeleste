## Science

Rhea is Saturn's second largest moon (about 1,530 km): a densely cratered body of ice and rock. Cassini found a very thin exosphere of oxygen and carbon dioxide around it, produced by particles from Saturn's magnetosphere striking the ice.

## History

Discovered by Giovanni Domenico Cassini on 23 December 1672. In 2008 Cassini recorded signals suggesting a ring system around Rhea: it would have been the first moon with rings. Later observations did not confirm them.

## Fun facts

- Rhea is Saturn's second largest moon but has only about 2% of Titan's mass.
- One of its youngest craters, Inktomi, is surrounded by bright rays of debris thrown out by the impact.
