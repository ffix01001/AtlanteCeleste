## Science

Hydra is the outermost of Pluto's known moons: an elongated body about 50 km long, with a very reflective surface of water ice. Like Nix, it tumbles chaotically.

## History

Discovered in 2005 with the Hubble Space Telescope. New Horizons photographed it in 2015.

## Fun facts

- The name is that of the many-headed monster that guarded the entrance to the underworld, fitting for Pluto's realm.
