## Science

Eros is a saddle-shaped near-Earth asteroid (NEO), 34 km long. Its surface is covered with boulders and dust, and it was the first asteroid studied at length by a probe in orbit.

## History

It was discovered on 13 August 1898 by Carl Gustav Witt in Berlin and, the same night, by Auguste Charlois in Nice. It was the first asteroid found near the Earth, and during its passes of 1900–1901 and 1931 it was used to measure the Earth–Sun distance precisely.

The NEAR Shoemaker probe entered orbit around Eros on 14 February 2000. On 12 February 2001, at the end of the mission, the team brought it slowly down onto the surface: even though it was not designed to land, it survived and kept transmitting. It was the first landing on an asteroid.

## Fun facts

- The probe arrived at the asteroid named after the god of love on Valentine's Day.
- In 2012 Eros passed about 27 million km from the Earth, visible with a small telescope.
