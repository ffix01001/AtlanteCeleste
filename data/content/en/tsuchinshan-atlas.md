## Science

C/2023 A3 (Tsuchinshan-ATLAS) was the great comet of autumn 2024. It passed perihelion on 27 September and reached its minimum distance from the Earth on 12 October 2024. In the following days it was visible to the naked eye in the evening sky, with a very long tail and a rare "anti-tail", a blade of dust that seemed to point towards the Sun because of a perspective effect.

## History

It was discovered on 9 January 2023 by the Purple Mountain Observatory (Tsuchinshan) in China and, independently, by the ATLAS network in South Africa the following month.

## Fun facts

- It comes from the Oort Cloud; after this passage it might be ejected from the Solar System and never return.
- The anti-tail can only be seen when the Earth crosses the plane of the comet's orbit.
