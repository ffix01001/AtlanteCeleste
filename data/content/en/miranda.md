## Science

Miranda is the smallest and innermost of the large moons of Uranus (about 470 km), but it has one of the most baffling surfaces in the Solar System: a "patchwork" of different terrains, with huge regions of concentric grooves called coronae. Verona Rupes, a cliff up to about 20 km high according to some estimates, is one of the tallest walls known. Recent studies suggest that Miranda might once have had an underground ocean.

## History

Discovered by Gerard Kuiper on 16 February 1948 at McDonald Observatory, in Texas. The only close-up image comes from Voyager 2 (1986), which photographed it on its way to Neptune.

## Fun facts

- The name comes from the daughter of the magician Prospero in Shakespeare's *The Tempest*.
- With its low gravity, a stone dropped from Verona Rupes would take several minutes to reach the bottom.
