## Science

Apophis is a near-Earth asteroid about 340 meters across. On 13 April 2029 it will pass about 32,000 km from the Earth's surface, closer than geostationary satellites: it will be visible to the naked eye from Europe and Africa, like a star moving across the sky. A close pass like this by an object of this size happens on average once every thousand years or so.

In 2021 new radar observations ruled out any risk of impact for at least the next hundred years.

## History

It was discovered on 19 June 2004 by Roy Tucker, David Tholen and the Italian Fabrizio Bernardi at the Kitt Peak observatory. In December 2004 the first estimates gave it a probability of impact in 2029 of up to about 2.7%: it is the only object ever to have reached level 4 on the Torino scale. Further observations quickly ruled out the impact.

NASA's OSIRIS-APEX probe will reach it right after the 2029 pass, to see how the Earth's gravity has changed it.

## Fun facts

- The name comes from Apep, the serpent of chaos in Egyptian mythology, but the discoverers were also fans of the series *Stargate SG-1*, where Apophis is a villain.
- During the 2029 pass the Earth's tidal forces might change its rotation and trigger small landslides on its surface.
