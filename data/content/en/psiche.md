## Science

Psyche is one of the largest asteroids in the main belt (about 280 × 230 × 190 km) and perhaps the strangest: radar and spectroscopic observations indicate that it is rich in metals, iron and nickel. It might be the exposed core of a protoplanet that lost its crust in violent collisions, a unique window into the interior of the rocky planets.

## History

It was discovered on 17 March 1852 by Annibale de Gasparis in Naples. NASA's Psyche probe, launched in 2023, is due to reach it in 2029.

## Fun facts

- It is often said that Psyche's metal "would be worth" billions of billions of euros: a fun calculation but one without any real economic meaning.
- In mythology Psyche was a mortal in love with Eros: both are asteroids.
