## Science

The Trifid Nebula (M20) is a cloud of gas and dust in Sagittarius, about 4,000 light years away. It is special because it brings together three types of nebula in a single object: the red part is hydrogen gas glowing in the light of a young, very hot star; the blue part is dust reflecting starlight; the black lanes are dust so dense that it hides what lies behind.

It is a very young region, a few hundred thousand years old. The Spitzer Space Telescope, observing in the infrared, found dozens of forming stars in the dark lanes, invisible in ordinary light.

## History

Charles Messier discovered it on 5 June 1764 and described it as a cluster of stars wrapped in nebulosity. The name "Trifid", that is divided into three, is due to John Herschel, who noticed the dark lanes that split it into three lobes.

## Fun facts

- The three dust lanes meet near the star that lights up the nebula, a multiple system of massive stars.
- In the sky it lies just over a degree from the Lagoon Nebula (M8).
