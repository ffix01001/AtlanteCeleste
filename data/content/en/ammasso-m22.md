## Science

M22 is one of the globular clusters closest to us, about 10,600 light years away in the constellation Sagittarius, and among the brightest in the sky: more than 70,000 old stars have been counted in it.

It has several peculiarities. It contains a planetary nebula, the shell of gas expelled by a dying star: only very few are known in all the globular clusters of the Galaxy. In 2012, observing with the VLA radio telescope, astronomers found two probable stellar-mass black holes in it, a surprise because globular clusters were thought to expel them.

## History

It was discovered in 1665 by the German astronomer Abraham Ihle: it is probably the first globular cluster ever discovered. Charles Messier cataloged it in 1764.

## Fun facts

- It lies near the ecliptic, the path of the Sun and the planets across the sky: every now and then the Moon and the planets pass close to it.
- From Italy, in summer, it is easy to see with binoculars just above the "teapot" of Sagittarius.
