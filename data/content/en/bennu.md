## Science

Bennu is a carbon-rich near-Earth asteroid about 500 meters across. It is one of the potentially most dangerous asteroids known: there is a probability of about 1 in 2,700 that it hits the Earth in September 2182.

The OSIRIS-REx probe discovered that its surface is anything but compact: when the sampling head touched it, in October 2020, it sank as if into a ball pit. Bennu also ejects small particles into space from time to time.

## History

Discovered in 1999 by the LINEAR program. On 24 September 2023 the OSIRIS-REx capsule landed in the Utah desert with about 120 grams of material. The analyses found 14 of the 20 amino acids used by life on Earth, all five bases of DNA and RNA, and salts formed by the evaporation of salty water.

## Fun facts

- The name was proposed by a 9-year-old boy in a contest: Bennu is a mythological Egyptian bird linked to rebirth.
- Bennu is spinning faster and faster, pushed by the weak pressure of sunlight re-emitted as heat (the YORP effect).
