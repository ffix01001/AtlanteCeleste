## Science

Lutetia is a main-belt asteroid about 120 km across, very old and cratered. Its composition suggests a primitive body, perhaps formed closer to the Sun and later moved into the belt.

## History

It was discovered on 15 November 1852 by Hermann Goldschmidt in Paris, and bears the city's Latin name. On 10 July 2010 the Rosetta probe flew past it at about 3,200 km, on its way to comet 67P.

## Fun facts

- Goldschmidt was a painter who did astronomy for passion: he discovered asteroids from the balcony of his home.
