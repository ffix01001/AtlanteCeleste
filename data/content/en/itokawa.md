## Science

Itokawa is a small near-Earth asteroid, about 535 meters long, shaped like a peanut. It is not a single block but a "rubble pile": boulders and gravel held together by very weak gravity.

## History

It was discovered in 1998 by the LINEAR program. The Japanese probe Hayabusa reached it in 2005 and, despite a long series of failures, in June 2010 brought back to Earth about 1,500 dust grains: the first samples ever collected from an asteroid.

## Fun facts

- The name honors Hideo Itokawa, the father of Japanese rocketry.
- The reentry of the Hayabusa capsule over the Australian desert was visible as an artificial shooting star.
