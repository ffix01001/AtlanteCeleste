## Science

Hera is the ESA mission that completes the planetary defense experiment begun by DART: it will study the double asteroid Didymos–Dimorphos up close to measure the mass of Dimorphos, the shape of the crater (or the deformation) left by the impact and how effectively the blow deflected the asteroid.

## History

It was launched on 7 October 2024. In March 2025 it flew past Mars and photographed the moon Deimos up close. Its arrival at the Didymos system is expected at the end of 2026.

## Fun facts

- It carries two small satellites: Juventas, with a radar to look inside Dimorphos, and the Italian Milani, which will study the dust. Milani is dedicated to the Pisan mathematician Andrea Milani, a pioneer of asteroid orbit computation.
- The name comes from the Greek goddess of marriage, wife of Zeus.
