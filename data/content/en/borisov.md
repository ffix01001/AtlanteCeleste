## Science

2I/Borisov is the second known interstellar object and the first certain interstellar comet: unlike ʻOumuamua it had a clearly visible coma and tail. Its gas was unusually rich in carbon monoxide, a sign that it formed in a very cold region around another star. It was traveling at about 32 km/s relative to the Sun.

## History

It was discovered on 30 August 2019 by Gennady Borisov, an amateur astronomer and observatory technician, with a 65 cm telescope he built himself at Nauchnyi, in Crimea. It reached perihelion on 8 December 2019.

## Fun facts

- It is one of the rare cases in which an important astronomical object bears the name of an amateur astronomer.
- Hubble saw its nucleus partially break apart in 2020.
