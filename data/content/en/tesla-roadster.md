## Science

Elon Musk's Tesla Roadster, with the dummy "Starman" in a spacesuit on board, travels on an orbit around the Sun that goes from just inside the Earth's orbit to beyond that of Mars, with a period of about 557 days. It is an inert object: no instruments, no communications.

## History

It was launched on 6 February 2018 as the demonstration payload of the first flight of the Falcon Heavy rocket, instead of the concrete block usually used in test flights. In January 2025 an amateur astronomer reported it as a new asteroid: the Minor Planet Center gave it the designation 2018 CN41, deleted the next day when it was realized it was the car.

## Fun facts

- On the dashboard is the message "DON'T PANIC!", a tribute to *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*; the stereo played David Bowie's *Space Oddity* until the batteries ran out.
- Solar radiation is probably degrading the paint and the plastic: what it looks like today is unknown.
