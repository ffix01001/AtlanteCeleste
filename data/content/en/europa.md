## Science

Beneath an ice crust perhaps 15–25 km thick, Europa hides a global ocean of salt water, tens of kilometers deep: it might contain more water than all the Earth's oceans put together. Warmed by Jupiter's tides and in contact with the rocky seafloor, it is one of the most promising places in the Solar System to look for life.

The surface is young and almost free of craters, crossed by long reddish fractures and by areas of "chaos terrain" where the ice seems to have broken up and frozen again. The Hubble telescope has observed possible plumes of water vapor.

## History

Discovered by Galileo on 8 January 1610. The Voyager probes and above all Galileo (1995–2003) revealed the icy crust and the magnetic evidence for the ocean. In 2003 the Galileo probe was deliberately crashed into Jupiter precisely to avoid contaminating Europa with microbes from Earth. Europa Clipper (NASA), launched in 2024, will arrive in 2030 and fly past it dozens of times; JUICE (ESA) will also observe it.
